## 3. Limitations

- **Bounded to three weekday pilot sites.** Every finding in this brief applies to weekday pilot sites Alder, Brook and Cove and nothing else [S1].
- **Weekend performance is excluded.** The charter states that observations cover weekdays only and that no weekend conclusion is supported [S1]. The reviewer note independently cautions that the pilot cannot establish weekend performance [S3].
- **Depot is excluded.** The Depot trial is explicitly excluded from the pilot's site list [S1], and the reviewer note cautions that the pilot cannot establish Depot outcomes [S3].
- **No wider rollout is claimed.** This brief claims no completed wider rollout; the reviewer's decision gate requires repeat measurement before any wider rollout is considered [S3].
- **No customer-level findings.** The observation log does not identify individual customers, so no customer-level conclusion is supported [S2].
- **No per-site medians exist to compare.** The baseline and pilot medians are single program-level figures [S1]; the medians in §2.7 are therefore not evidence of any site-level difference, and no per-site median comparison is made in this brief.
- **No cross-site volume comparison.** The observation log states no volume-control or comparability condition [S2], so this brief presents delayed-acknowledgement counts per site as reported and does not normalize or rank them.
- **Attribution of the median change is not isolated.** The charter records that routing runbook version 2 replaced version 1 mid-window on 2026-01-12 [S1] and reports the baseline and pilot medians only as single program-level figures [S1]. Because the sources report no medians split by runbook version, this brief does not attribute the 18-to-11-minute movement to routing alone or to any other single cause; that abstention is this brief's own reading of what the sources omit, not a statement the sources make.
- **Sample size is bounded.** The independent control is 60 acknowledgement records per site, 180 in total [S2], which the reviewer confirms reconciles to the observation totals [S3]; the brief makes no claim beyond that reconciliation.
- **No derived rates or projections.** This brief reports counts and medians as stated in the sources and computes no rates, percentages or forecasts.
