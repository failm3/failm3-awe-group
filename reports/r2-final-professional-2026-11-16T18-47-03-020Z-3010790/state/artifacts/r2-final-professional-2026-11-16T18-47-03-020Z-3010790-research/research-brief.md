## (a) Executive Recommendation

**Adopt the reviewer's proposal as a bounded, measured two-week extension: carry Alder's routing practices into Brook and Cove for two weeks, and do not authorize any wider rollout until that repeat measurement has been taken and reviewed.**

This recommendation rests on three things the sources actually establish, and on nothing else.

1. The independent reviewer explicitly recommends it: "extend Alder routing practices to Brook and Cove for two weeks" [S3].
2. The same note sets the condition in the same breath: "Decision gate: repeat measurement before any wider rollout" [S3]. The extension is therefore the measurement step, not the rollout.
3. The pilot data are internally consistent and checkable. All three sites recorded 480 requests over 4 site-weeks each [S2], and the independent reviewer's 180 sampled records "reconcile to the weekly observation totals" [S3].

The comparative basis for choosing Alder as the reference site is narrow and should be stated as narrowly as it exists. The reviewer records that "Alder has the smallest delayed-acknowledgement count and the fewest escalations" [S3]. Against that, Alder recorded 31 delayed acknowledgements and 2 supervisor escalations, Cove 37 and 3, and Brook 43 and 4, each over 480 requests and 4 site-weeks [S2]. Those three counts support the reviewer's ordering; they do not explain it, and the sources offer no causal account.

The program-level acknowledgement median moved from 18 minutes manual to 11 minutes routed [S1]. That is the strongest single quantitative signal in the sources, and it is also the one most easily overstated: the charter states it for the pilot as a whole, provides no per-site median, and does not attribute the change to Alder, Brook or Cove individually [S1]. It should be treated as program-level context for the extension, not as an expected per-site outcome.

**What this recommendation does not authorize:** weekend conclusions, Depot conclusions, or any completed wider rollout. See (c) and (f).

## (c) Limitations

These are material to the recommendation and must travel with it.

1. **Weekday-only coverage.** "observations cover weekdays only; no weekend or Depot conclusion is supported" [S1]. No finding here speaks to weekend operation at any site.
2. **Depot is excluded by design.** "the Depot trial is explicitly excluded" [S1], and the reviewer independently cautions that "this pilot cannot establish weekend performance or Depot outcomes" [S3]. No Depot evidence exists in these sources.
3. **No completed wider rollout.** The recommendation in [S3] is prospective. No source states that the two-week extension to Brook and Cove was approved, executed or completed, and no source records a wider rollout [S3]. The decision gate remains open: "repeat measurement before any wider rollout" [S3].
4. **The acknowledgement median change is program-level only.** [S1] reports 18 minutes baseline and 11 minutes pilot for the program as a whole and provides no per-site medians. The change cannot be attributed to Alder, Brook or Cove individually [S1].
5. **No causal attribution.** The sources report counts and a program-level median shift. They give no reason why Alder's delayed-acknowledgement count is lower than Cove's or Brook's, and none is inferred here [S2], [S3].
6. **Mid-window runbook change.** Routing runbook version 2 replaced version 1 on 2026-01-12, inside the 2026-01-05 to 2026-01-30 window [S1]. The sources provide no before/after split, so the influence of the runbook change on any of the 31/43/37 counts or on the median change cannot be isolated [S1], [S2].
7. **No per-week breakdown.** Each site's delayed-acknowledgement figure is a single aggregate across the four-week window, with no week-by-week series [S2]. Trend direction within the window cannot be established for any site [S2].
8. **Supervisor escalations are counted but undefined.** The counts (Alder 2, Brook 4, Cove 3) are reported [S2], but no source defines what triggers an escalation or what level would be acceptable. They are not comparable to any stated benchmark [S2].
9. **Sampling coverage is 60 records per site, not the full record set.** The independent control covers 180 sampled records [S2], [S3], which reconcile to the weekly totals [S3]; reconciliation is not a full re-audit of every record.
10. **No customer-level analysis is possible.** "the log does not identify individual customers" [S2].
11. **The 120 requests per site-week is a charter baseline whose measurement method is not specified** [S1]. The observed 480 per site is the operative figure [S2].
12. **Results are uncontrolled and non-randomized.** No source describes a comparison group, a control period, or randomization between runbook versions or sites [S1], [S2].
