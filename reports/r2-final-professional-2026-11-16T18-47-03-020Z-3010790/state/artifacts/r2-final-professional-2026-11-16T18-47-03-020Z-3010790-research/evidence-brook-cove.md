## 8. Limitations and exclusions found in the sources

1. **Weekday-only coverage; no weekend conclusion supported.** Observations cover weekdays only, and the charter states that no weekend or Depot conclusion is supported [S1]. Nothing here can speak to weekend performance at Brook or Cove.
2. **Depot explicitly out of scope.** The Depot trial is excluded by name in the charter [S1], and the reviewer independently cautions that the pilot cannot establish Depot outcomes [S3]. No Depot finding is available or implied.
3. **Reviewer caution restated.** The reviewer's caution is explicit: "this pilot cannot establish weekend performance or Depot outcomes" [S3].
4. **No missing weekday records, but no individual customer identification.** The log reports "no missing weekday records" and separately states that "the log does not identify individual customers" [S2]. Completeness is therefore asserted for weekdays only, and no per-customer analysis is possible from these sources.
5. **Mid-window runbook change.** Routing runbook version 2 replaced version 1 on 2026-01-12, inside the 2026-01-05 to 2026-01-30 window [S1]. The sources give no before/after split of results, so the effect of the runbook change on any figure — including Brook's 43 and Cove's 37 — cannot be isolated.
6. **No per-week or per-site breakdown of delayed acknowledgements.** [S2] reports one aggregate figure per site for the whole four-week window (Brook 43, Cove 37) with no week-by-week series. Trend direction within the window, for either site, **cannot be established from these sources** [S2].
7. **Escalations counted but no threshold defined.** Supervisor escalations are reported per site (Brook 4, Cove 3) [S2], but no source defines what triggers an escalation or what count would be considered acceptable. The counts are not comparable to any stated benchmark.
8. **Median change is program-wide with no per-site medians.** The 18-to-11-minute acknowledgement median change is stated for the program as a whole [S1]. There are no per-site medians, so no site-specific median improvement — for Brook, Cove or Alder — can be asserted [S1].
9. **The recommendation is prospective, not a result.** [S3] recommends extending Alder routing practices to Brook and Cove for two weeks and sets a decision gate to repeat measurement before any wider rollout. No source records that this recommendation was adopted, that the extension ran, or that any wider rollout occurred.
10. **No causal attribution available.** The sources report counts and a program-level median change; they contain no information identifying why Brook's delayed-acknowledgement count differs from Cove's, and none is inferred here.
