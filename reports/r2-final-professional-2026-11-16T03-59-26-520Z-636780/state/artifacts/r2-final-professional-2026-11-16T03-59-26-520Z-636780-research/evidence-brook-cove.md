## Limitations / Do-not-infer

### Do not infer weekend performance
- The log records are weekday records — the data-quality statement speaks only of weekday records [S2].
- The reviewer states the pilot cannot establish weekend performance [S3].
- **No weekend conclusion may be drawn for Brook, Cove or any other site from this note.**

### Do not infer Depot outcomes
- The reviewer states the pilot cannot establish Depot outcomes [S3].
- Neither S2 nor S3 reports any Depot figure, and this note makes no claim about Depot.
- **No Depot conclusion may be drawn from this note.**

### Do not infer a completed rollout
- The reviewer's text is a recommendation to extend Alder routing practices to Brook and Cove for two weeks, plus a decision gate requiring repeat measurement before any wider rollout [S3].
- A recommendation and a gate are not an implemented change. No sentence in S2 or S3 states that the extension was adopted, that it ran, that it was measured, or that any wider rollout occurred [S3].
- **No completed-rollout, in-progress-rollout or post-rollout-outcome claim is supported by this note.**

### Do not over-read the Brook-vs-Cove comparison
- The reviewer does not rank Brook against Cove [S3]; the only ordering stated is that Alder is lowest on delayed acknowledgements and escalations [S3].
- The recorded counts (Brook 43 delays / 4 escalations; Cove 37 delays / 3 escalations) [S2] do not by themselves establish a cause, a trend or a service-level difference. Any difference stated above is labelled derived arithmetic on cited figures, not a sourced finding [S2].
- These totals cover four site-weeks and cannot be extrapolated to other periods, sites or conditions [S2][S3].

### Do not treat the review as a full audit
- The review reconciled a 60-per-site sample to the weekly totals [S2][S3]. That is a sample reconciliation, not a record-level audit of every acknowledgement.
- The log does not identify individual customers [S2], so no customer-segment, repeat-contact or individual-case inference is available from these sources.

### Do not attribute causality
- No sentence in S2 or S3 attributes the recorded delayed-acknowledgement counts to a routing change, a runbook change, staffing, or any other cause [S2][S3]. This note makes no causal claim.

### Figures are as recorded, not recomputed
- The 480, 43, 37, 4 and 3 figures are transcribed as recorded [S2]. Any arithmetic shown is labelled as derived and adds no evidentiary weight beyond its cited inputs.
