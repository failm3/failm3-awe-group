# Northstar service reliability pilot: decision brief

Prepared by: research-coordinator, mission r2-final-professional-2026-11-16T20-21-29-437Z-3269230-research.
Evidence base: sources S1, S2 and S3 only (see Sources). Child evidence notes retained as attributed artifacts: `evidence-alder.md` (researcher, Alder) and `evidence-brook-cove.md` (researcher, Brook and Cove). All figures below were checked against the three source files.

## 1. Executive recommendation

- Extend Alder's routing practices to Brook and Cove for two weeks, as the independent reviewer recommends. [S3]
- Repeat the measurement before any wider rollout. The reviewer sets this as the decision gate. [S3]
- Do not treat this brief as approval of a wider rollout. The sources describe a three-site weekday pilot, and no wider rollout is reported as completed. [S1] [S3]
- Basis: Alder had the smallest delayed-acknowledgement count (31) and the fewest escalations (2). [S2] [S3] The pilot acknowledgement median was 11 minutes against an 18-minute manual baseline. [S1]
- Scope bound: the conclusions apply only to the weekday pilot sites Alder, Brook and Cove. Weekend operation and the Depot site are excluded from any generalization. [S1] [S3]

## 2. Factual findings

### 2.1 Site totals and delayed acknowledgements

| Site | Site-weeks | Requests | Delayed acknowledgements | Supervisor escalations | Citation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Alder | 4 | 480 | 31 | 2 | [S2] |
| Brook | 4 | 480 | 43 | 4 | [S2] |
| Cove | 4 | 480 | 37 | 3 | [S2] |

- Each of the three sites has a logged request total of 480. [S2]
- Delayed acknowledgements are 31 for Alder, 43 for Brook and 37 for Cove. [S2]
- A delayed acknowledgement is an elapsed acknowledgement time greater than 15 minutes, measured after routing. [S2]
- The independent reviewer states that Alder has the smallest delayed-acknowledgement count and the fewest escalations. [S3]

### 2.2 Acknowledgement timing

- The baseline manual acknowledgement median is 18 minutes. [S1]
- The pilot routed acknowledgement median is 11 minutes, so the median changed from 18 to 11 minutes. [S1]
- Acknowledgement uses receipt timestamps. [S1]
- The charter states these medians once, at program level. No source gives a site-specific median. [S1] [S2] [S3]

### 2.3 Pilot design

- The pilot window is 2026-01-05 through 2026-01-30, four operating weeks. [S1]
- The pilot sites are Alder, Brook and Cove. The Depot trial is explicitly excluded. [S1]
- The baseline is 120 requests per site-week. [S1]
- Routing runbook version 2 replaced version 1 on 2026-01-12. [S1]
- The program owner is Service Operations. [S1]

### 2.4 Data quality and review

- One independent reviewer sampled 60 acknowledgement records per site. [S2]
- The reviewer reports that the 180 sampled records reconcile to the weekly observation totals. [S3]
- The log has no missing weekday records and does not identify individual customers. [S2]

## 3. Limitations

- Weekdays only. Observations cover weekdays, and no weekend or Depot conclusion is supported. [S1] [S3]
- No wider rollout is established. The reviewer requires repeat measurement before any wider rollout. [S3]
- The 18-to-11-minute change is a program-level figure. The sources do not attribute it to Alder, Brook or Cove individually. [S1]
- No cause is established. The sources do not say whether the routed acknowledgement, the runbook change or any other factor produced the site differences in delayed acknowledgements or escalations. [S1] [S2] [S3]
- The runbook changed on 2026-01-12, inside the window. No source splits results before and after that date or by runbook version. [S1] [S2]
- No delay rates are stated. Only counts are given against the 480-request totals. [S2]
- The sources do not describe what "Alder routing practices" consist of, and they give no success criteria for the repeat measurement. [S3]
- The sources do not say whether the baseline used the same 15-minute delay threshold or the same measurement as the pilot. The baseline is a manual median and the pilot is a routed median. [S1] [S2]
- The 180-record sample is stated by S3 and the 60-per-site sampling by S2. Neither source gives a per-site reconciliation. [S2] [S3]
- The reviewer note is dated 2026-02-03 and the log is revision 2026-02-02. No source states that the reviewer worked from that exact revision. [S2] [S3]
- No discrepancy in figures was found between S1, S2 and S3. The child evidence notes list the open questions above. [S1] [S2] [S3]
